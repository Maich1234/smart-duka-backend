# Price-review export layout, 31 October 2026

The export now opens with the complete current product/variant anomaly register. Each entry includes issue reasons, selling and purchase prices, stock, per-unit shortfall, potential stock shortfall, optional flexible/bundle information and the recorded price editor. Definitions and any historical sales shortfalls follow. The signed totals and a mandatory, clickable validation QR close the report. A one-product report fits on one page with its summary toward the bottom. Larger reports retain all rows and close with the summary on the final page.

Rows are measured at their actual wrapped height. Exceptionally long legacy names can continue across pages with repeated document headers. Numbered footers reserve their own area; writing them does not create extra pages. Partial product lists reject rather than silently creating a report with incomplete details. The existing owner export endpoint still requests up to 10,000 alerts independently of screen pagination; it rejects larger exports explicitly. The QR verifies signed summary totals and currency, not every row or an independent audit opinion.

Independent checks used synthetic one-product, 27-product, empty and exceptionally long-name report fixtures. Generated and visually reviewed first/final page images, checked page bounds and footer numbering, all product names and product-before-summary ordering. The rendered QR decoded to its clickable URL, signed totals/currency verified, and a changed signature rejected. Actual Express/JWT owner HTTP export with model/service adapters confirmed full report options despite page 9 / limit 1, staff 403, oversized/incomplete 422 and private no-store PDF responses. The actual HTTP PDF contained all 27 products before the summary. No existing suite, customer record write or live provider call was used. These checks do not prove real Mongo aggregation behavior or physical phone sharing.

The companion mobile follow-up explicitly registers price-alerts with the outer navigator header suppressed and href:null. PriceAlertsScreen already draws one accessible header. The earlier merged UI redesign supplies Inter typography, compact metrics/actions and the explanation disclosure. No backend or mobile production merge/deployment was performed by this follow-up.
